# 📊 Blinkit Sales Analysis – Visualization-Based Assignment

## 📌 Project Overview
This project is a **business analysis assignment** based on a set of questions provided in a PPT.  
The objective was **not to build a dashboard or use any specific BI tool**, but to **answer business questions using appropriate data visualizations**.

Each visualization is selected purely based on:
- The business question
- The KPI involved
- The type of comparison or relationship required

---

## 🎯 Business Requirement
To analyze **Blinkit’s sales performance, customer satisfaction, and inventory distribution** and answer specific business questions using **visual representations only**.

The focus of this assignment is on:
- Understanding business requirements
- Selecting the right visualization for each question
- Interpreting KPIs correctly

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The analysis is centered around the following KPIs:

- **Total Sales**  
  → Overall revenue generated from all items sold.

- **Average Sales**  
  → Average revenue per sale.

- **Number of Items**  
  → Total count of different items sold.

- **Average Rating**  
  → Average customer rating of items sold.

---

## 📊 Visualization-Based Questions & Solutions

### 1️⃣ Total Sales by Fat Content
- **Business Question:**  
  How does fat content impact total sales?
- **Reasoning:**  
  Fat content is a categorical variable, and the goal is to understand contribution.
- **Chosen Visualization:**  
  Donut Chart
- **KPIs Considered:**  
  Total Sales, Average Sales, Number of Items, Average Rating

---

### 2️⃣ Total Sales by Item Type
- **Business Question:**  
  Which item types contribute the most to total sales?
- **Reasoning:**  
  Comparison across multiple categories requires clear magnitude comparison.
- **Chosen Visualization:**  
  Bar Chart
- **KPIs Considered:**  
  Total Sales, Average Sales, Number of Items, Average Rating

---

### 3️⃣ Fat Content by Outlet for Total Sales
- **Business Question:**  
  How does fat content influence sales across different outlets?
- **Reasoning:**  
  Requires comparison across outlets while showing segmentation.
- **Chosen Visualization:**  
  Stacked Column Chart
- **KPIs Considered:**  
  Total Sales, Average Sales, Number of Items, Average Rating

---

### 4️⃣ Total Sales by Outlet Establishment
- **Business Question:**  
  Does outlet establishment type or age influence total sales?
- **Reasoning:**  
  Time or sequence-based trends are best visualized using continuous charts.
- **Chosen Visualization:**  
  Line Chart

---

### 5️⃣ Sales by Outlet Size
- **Business Question:**  
  How does outlet size relate to total sales?
- **Reasoning:**  
  Proportional contribution analysis suits circular charts.
- **Chosen Visualization:**  
  Donut / Pie Chart

---

### 6️⃣ Sales by Outlet Location
- **Business Question:**  
  How are sales distributed across different locations?
- **Reasoning:**  
  Location-based comparison requires geographic or flow-style visualization.
- **Chosen Visualization:**  
  bar chart

---

## 🧠 Key Learnings
- How to translate business questions into analytical questions
